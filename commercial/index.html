Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Titusville HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts and the stuffy dining room fast. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Titusville HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Titusville HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Titusville and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
