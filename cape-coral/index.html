Keeping Your Cape Coral HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install in peak season.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement.
